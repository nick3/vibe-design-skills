## Known limitations retained for stable v1.0

- No proposed cross-layer visual system has been rendered and compared across
  representative products.
- No complete Agentic UI scenario has exercised real authorization, progress,
  fallback, recovery, or renderer behavior end to end.
- Browser, device, accessibility, production, and rollback tools were not
  exercised by the reconstruction fixture.
- Independent human product/UI designers have not reviewed the suite against
  the stable acceptance criteria in `skills/VALIDATION.md`.
- Nondeterministic routing must be re-evaluated whenever descriptions or
  precedence rules change.

These are tracked maturity requirements, not hidden public-preview blockers.
